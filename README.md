
# Excel Capstone Project - Healthcare Data Insights and Dashboarding

## 📌 Project Overview
This dashboard, titled "Healthcare Dashboard - Analysis", acts as a visual analytics tool designed to track patient demographics, 
health risk factors (specifically weight status and smoking habits), and their associated financial impacts.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Financial Performance Tracking: To aggregate and display total healthcare expenditures (Sum of Charges) alongside individual cost metrics (Average of Charges).

- Risk-Factor Correlation: To analyze how physical health indicators—specifically Weight Status—impact the overall volume of healthcare charges.

- Demographic & Lifestyle Profiling: To evaluate the intersection of lifestyle choices and medical histories, specifically highlighting the proportion of smokers with a history of cancer versus non-smokers.

- Regional & Segment Analysis: To provide dynamic slicing capabilities (via the left-hand filters) so healthcare administrators can drill down into specific states or weight categories to find localized trends.

 
Key Findings 

By analyzing the specific charts within the dashboard, we can draw the following conclusions:

- Obesity is the Primary Driver of Healthcare Costs
    The bottom bar chart, "Weight Status Total Charges," clearly demonstrates that patients categorized under "Obesity" generate the overwhelming majority of healthcare costs.

    Obesity: ~$2,04,99,520.17 (By far the highest financial burden)

    Overweight: ~$56,36,436.60

    Normal Weight: ~$42,42,121.56

    Underweight: ~$12,14,279.88


- Cancer History Among Smokers is a Significant Minority

  The pie chart titled "Cancer History Smokers vs Non Smokers" shows the distribution of cancer histories.

  83% of the observed demographic do not have a recorded history of cancer.

  17% do have a recorded history of cancer.

- Resource Utilization is Uniform Across the Current View

  The top-left summary bar chart shows an identical count for "No. Of Customer" (2,335) and "No Of Hospital" (2,335).
